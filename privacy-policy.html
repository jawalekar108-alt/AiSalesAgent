Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Sales Automation ("we", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and its integration with Instagram (via Facebook Login and the Instagram Graph API).

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram Business/Creator account through Facebook Login, we may collect:

  • Basic profile information (name, username, profile picture)
  • Instagram Page/Account ID and access tokens
  • Instagram media (posts, captions, media URLs, comments) that you authorize us to access
  • Page insights/metrics related to your connected Instagram Business account
  • Contact information you provide directly (e.g. email address)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your Instagram posts and content inside our React Native mobile application
  • To power AI-based sales automation features (e.g. auto-replies, lead capture, content suggestions)
  • To authenticate you and maintain your session securely
  • To improve and troubleshoot the App's functionality

3. Data Storage & Security

Access tokens and account data are stored securely and are used only to communicate with the Instagram Graph API on your behalf. We do not sell your personal data. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your Instagram data with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • With service providers strictly necessary to operate the App (e.g. hosting, AI processing), under confidentiality obligations

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the App's features or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details below, or by removing the App's access from your Instagram/Facebook account settings.

6. Your Rights

  • You can disconnect your Instagram account from the App at any time
  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • You can request correction or deletion of your data

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
